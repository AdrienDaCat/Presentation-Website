Backstory

sentience of humans who could help advance medical research came into play. Therefore, with eleven volenteers,
and twenty scientists on the team, the Sentient Cells Project was formed.

With the success of the Sentient Cells Project, scientists have begun study of these creatures and how they
interact with their minerature world.

“Everything Is Fine” Route

  • Befriend all of your neighbors and resolve any conflict you come across.
  • Avoid getting in trouble with the police when supplying Mug with the materials to commit murder and get away with it.
  • Avoid creating enemies in your neighborhood.
  • Do not lose your sanity in the final minigame while helping Mug commit a crime.

“Everything is NOT Fine” Route

  • Befriend all of your neighbors and resolve any conflict you come across.
  • Avoid getting in trouble with the police when supplying Mug with the materials to commit murder and get away with it.
  • Avoid creating enemies in your neighborhood.
  • Lose all of your sanity in the final minigame while helping Mug commit a murder and get away with it. — Run
    away from your partner and leave them alone… forever.

“Maybe We’ll Be Fine. . .” Route

  • Befriend all of your neighbors and resolve any conflict you come across.
  • Avoid getting in trouble with the police when supplying Mug with the materials to commit murder and get away with it.
  • Avoid creating enemies in your neighborhood.
  • Lose all of your sanity in the final minigame while helping Mug commit a murder and get away with it. — Run away
    WITH your partner and live hiding from the law together.

“They Got Away With Murder” Route

  • Any other actions do not matter here.
  • Try to expose Mug to the police without enough evidence and get killed off by them.

“Town Hero” Route

  • Befriend all of your neighbors and resolve any conflict you come across. Avoid creating enemies in your neighborhood.
  • Gather enough evidence and report Mug to the police.

“YOU FILTHY RAT!!” Route

  • Do NOT befriend all of your neighbors and do NOT resolve any conflict you come across.
  • Avoid making friends with anyone, creating enemies is optional.
  • Gather enough evidence and report Mug to the police.

“Life Without You. . .” Route

  • Meet Mug but avoid him for the rest of the game.
  • Do NOT befriend any of your neighbors and do NOT resolve conflict.
  • Find Mot dead at the end of the game

“This Town Has Secrets…” Route

  • Meet Mug but avoid him for the rest of the game.
  • Befriend all of your neighbors and help resolve all conflicts.
  • Close the game with a happy ending with Mot, wondering about the recent murders.

”What if you woke up, and everything was gone? - Chapter One

“What if you woke up, and everything was gone?”

“Excuse me?”

That question lingered in their mind, as they stood in the ruins of the old police office. Broken windows that allowed
sunlight to cast down into the dark room in golden strands. Chairs thrown around as the smell of rot and decay burned
their nostrils. Power in the town had been out for months, no one was around to ensure they stayed on.

“What if you woke up, and everything was gone?”

That question burned into their mind. Nagging at them at every step they took through the forgotten town.

Charlie Harvey dug into their black cargo pants pocket, pulling out a small booklet. Opening the cover and flipping through
several pages, they reached the current month. Something their mother taught them at the start of this apocalyptic hellscape
was to keep track of the days. To mark down how long it has been. They didn’t know why they needed to know, but keeping track
of the days somehow kept them sane. Even after watching horrors that took place within the last six months.

Half a year since they came here.

Half a year since the world ended.

They caused this. They came down from the clouds and brought a twisted and mangled human specimen. Then it all went downhill from
there. Nothing ever really goes uphill when people are clawing and biting into each other, ripping the meat off the bone like a wild animal.

Charlie lifted themself onto the front desk, slipping behind the broken glass windows and carefully landing behind the desk. On their
belt, they pulled out a small flashlight, one that had gone through many batteries while they explored the wasteland.

They didn’t find themself worried about getting their head bit off, not anymore. Most of everything was rotted and unable to travel a few
inches let alone chase them down for miles.

You’d think a country that took the safety of its citizens seriously would prevent even the smallest of towns from becoming unrecognizable
ruins. It started with a few news reports, then stations were down in all the major cities, and then the country went dark. Shortly following
the United States was the rest of the world.

Charlie side stepped around puddles of glass, even with their thick boots they didn’t want to risk an injury from the glass shards. They were
here for old supplies, not more injuries that would hinder their survival.

What were they even surviving for?

The hope that if they made it to a certain point the world would magically be fixed? That they would see their dead family again? Or was it to
see the face of Jennifer Carter smiling at them once again. They were going to ask them to homecoming the week before the world ended. But stalling
did them no good. It only made their attempts now impossible.

They pushed open the door, already about to give out on its hinges. They instantly pinched their nose, an old police man with his mouth open a gap laid
on the floor in a wakeless nap. They carefully crouched down and reached down for his belt, shuffling a gun out of the hoister, grabbing the flashlight,
along with it.

This place already looked like most of the other possible resources were snatched up from the beginning of this event. At least they could return to their
bunker quickly.

They started the way they came, hopping the counter and rushing for the glass doors. They began their quick pace down the old highway road, racing past
burnt out cars and smashed vehicles. Flies happily feasted on the remains of society, providing an irritating background noise that bugged Charlie to no end.

Slowing their pace to a stop, they stood in the center of the street, turning on their heels to make sure there wasn’t the possibility of survivors around.

Of course not. No one has been alive in months.

They began their journey through the parking lot. It’d come to no shock that they would choose the grocery store of all places to hide. Food available for
months, running water, and most importantly it had supplies they could use, and was close to other potential supply hoards.

They slipped through the broken automated doors, walking through the maze of aisles to the back of the store. In the electronics section they had a lot of
mattresses and pillows from the furniture section piled up and organized for bedding. Clothing neatly stacked in piles, and the electronics cleared out stacked
into walls like a barrier. A much better purpose in this offline world.

Being able to go online and talk on the internet, that was one of their favorite things when they got home from school. They missed their online friends. They
wondered if they were okay, or even still alive. Surely not, surely they had perished along with the lives of billions of other individuals.

Charlie slipped down onto the stack of mattresses, dropping their new finds on the bed next to them. They huffed out a long sigh, rubbing the palms of their hands
against their face and throwing themself backwards. They felt gross right now. The dirt and grime, and the sweat building up behind their knees and in their elbows.
It bothered them badly, they disliked it. It felt utterly disgusting. They had yet to shower in weeks, and they knew that wasn’t good for them. In their defense, you
don’t exactly feel the need to worry about said objective while you’re on the edge of extinction.

Part of them believed they should not even still be alive. Why is it them who had survived for so long? Why not a real adult who was actually experienced in life and
knew what they were doing when the world was broken.

Tears swelled up in their eyes, their nose stuffed up as they tried to rub their eyes clear of tears. Yet they wouldn’t stop.

They sobbed, as loud as they could.

Who would hear them?

Who would comfort them?

They wished they had their mom to hug their neck and tell them everything would be okay. Who would take their hand and guide them through their fears until they
reached a satisfactory end.

The world felt like it was on fire, and they were the only one to truly experience it. Witnessing their mom die by the dreadful teeth of the misfortunate who found
themself infected.

They missed their friends, who would make them joke about even the worst of times and make the most terrifying incidents have light passing through them.

They missed everyone.

They wanted them back.

Someone bring them back…

Bring their life back.

Give them their life back.

They graduated this year. They will never get to walk in front of the graduating class and accept their diploma. Accept their next chapter in life.

Their hand reached towards the side of the bed, their fingers danced around the trigger of the gun, retrieving the weapon and pulling it closer to themself. Pulling
the hammer back on the back of the gun they slowly pulled it closer. Their eyes admiring the beauty of the item before bringing the barrel closer… and closer… and..

CRASH!

They shot up from their sulking rest, eyes wide and heart pounding. Something had been knocked over…
